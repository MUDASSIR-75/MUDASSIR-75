## 🚀 About Me

I'm a passionate **AI Engineer** and **LLM Specialist** with expertise in **Full Stack AI Application Development**. I specialize in building production-grade, domain-specific AI solutions that solve real-world problems across healthcare, education, and enterprise sectors.

As the founder of **[ENDEVSOLS](https://github.com/ENDEVSOLS)** (a Pakistan-based private limited company), I lead the development of innovative AI applications and contribute actively to the open-source community, including **LangChain** integrations.

### 🎯 What I Do
- 🎨 **Full-Stack Development**: Building modern web applications with React.js, Next.js, and TypeScript
- 🤖 **AI Agents & Chatbots**: Building intelligent conversational systems with memory and reasoning capabilities
- 🧠 **RAG Systems**: Designing advanced retrieval-augmented generation pipelines for enterprise knowledge bases
- 🔗 **LangChain/LangGraph**: Implementing complex AI workflows and multi-agent systems
- ☁️ **Cloud Architecture**: Deploying scalable AI solutions on AWS with proper DevOps practices
- 🏥 **Domain-Specific AI**: HIPAA/GDPR-compliant healthcare systems and specialized educational platforms
